Hello! I am an Assistant Professor at the Université de Sherbrooke (QC, Canada). My research focuses broadly on the prediction and modeling of aerodynamic, aeroacoustic, and aeroelasticity phenomena in turbomachinery, from drones to large aircraft turbofan engines. 

I did my PhD between the <a href="https://www.usherbrooke.ca">Université de Sherbrooke</a> and <a href="https://www.isae-supaero.fr/en/">ISAE-Supaero</a> in Toulouse (France), working under the supervision of Stéphane Moreau, Marc C. Jacob, and Michaël Bauerheim. Before that, I obtained a Bachelor's degree in Aerospace Engineering at the Universidad Politécnica de Madrid (UPM) and a MsC at ISAE-Supaero.

I'm a fan of high-fidelity Computational Fluid Dynamics solvers, especially open-source ones (e.g. <a href="https://palabos.unige.ch/">Palabos</a>, a Lattice-Boltzmann solver from U. Geneva), but also of analytical models (potential aerodynamics, aeroacoustic models). I use such approaches to feed data-driven models (Neural Networks, Gaussian Processes, or others), aiming to develop general and robust predictors of unsteady phenomena (turbulence, acoustics, elasticity) in realistic turbomachinery applications.
